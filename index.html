Our Story

Greeta AI was born from a simple frustration: too many great local businesses were losing customers to missed calls, slow responses, and the impossible cost of staffing a front desk 24 hours a day. We knew there had to be a better way.

Powered by ulio.ai and built for the San Diego market, Greeta AI gives every business — from local contractors to medical offices to restaurants — an intelligent, always-on receptionist that answers every call, books every appointment, and never has a bad day.

We don't just sell software. We sit down with you, demo the technology live, and build something that fits your specific business. Then we're with you every step of the way.
